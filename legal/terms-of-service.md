## 3. Citation Requirements for Primary Sources

Islam.wiki prioritizes accuracy and scholarly integrity. When contributing content that references primary Islamic sources (Quran, Hadith collections, classical fiqh texts), you must:

- Cite the source by recognized name (e.g., Sahih al-Bukhari, Sunan Abu Dawud) and include the hadith number or ayah reference where applicable.
- Not attribute fabricated, weak (da'if), or disputed narrations as sahih without explicit notation of their grading.
- Use recognized hadith grading terminology (sahih, hasan, da'if, mawdu') and attribute the grading to a recognized scholar.

Content that misrepresents hadith authenticity may be corrected, annotated, or removed by our moderation team.
