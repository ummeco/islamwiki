## Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** May 1, 2026
**Last reviewed:** 2026-04-26
**Version:** 1.0.0

This policy applies to Islam.wiki at islam.wiki (and sunnah.co / sunnah.us redirects),
operated by Ummeco LLC. For all base terms, see the shared base policy. The sections below
describe what is specific to Islam.wiki.

Questions? Email privacy@islam.wiki.

---

### 1. Who We Are

Ummeco LLC operates Islam.wiki, an open Islamic knowledge base available at islam.wiki.

**Privacy contact:** privacy@islam.wiki
**GDPR data subject requests:** dpa@ummat.dev

---

### 2. Search Query Logs (Islam.wiki-Specific)

When you use the search feature on Islam.wiki, we log your search query to improve relevance.

- Search queries are retained for **30 days** and then permanently deleted
- After 30 days, aggregate statistics (e.g., "top 100 queries this month") may be retained
  with no link to individual users or sessions
- We do not link search queries to your account unless you are signed in and have opted in
  to search history in account settings
- We do not share search queries with any third party
- You can disable search logging entirely by turning off "Analytics cookies" in your
  Cookie Preferences

---

### 3. Content You Submit (Islam.wiki-Specific)

Islam.wiki collects:

- Search queries (see above)
- Optional user contributions: article edits, citations submitted, corrections flagged
- Bookmarks and reading lists (if you create an account)
- Feedback and comments submitted via the feedback form

User contributions to articles are associated with your account username (which may be a
pseudonym). Contributions are publicly visible unless you withdraw them; withdrawal removes
your username association but may leave the content visible per the wiki's editorial policy.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy

Islam.wiki is a general Islamic knowledge reference. It does not target children. The service
is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from
children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data without parental consent, contact
privacy@islam.wiki and we will delete it promptly.

---

### 5. Data Retention (Islam.wiki Additions)

| Data class | Retention | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Search query logs (raw) | 30 days, then deleted | Relevance tuning |
| Search aggregate statistics | 12 months | Trend analysis, no user link |
| Article contribution history | Indefinite (editorial record) | Wiki integrity |
| Bookmarks / reading lists | Until account deleted | User preference |
